Essential information about Indian stock market structure, indices, sectors, and trading rules.

## Market Structure

### Exchanges

| Exchange | Full Name | Key Index | Website |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NSE** | National Stock Exchange | NIFTY 50 | nseindia.com |
| **BSE** | Bombay Stock Exchange | SENSEX | bseindia.com |

- NSE is the larger exchange by volume
- BSE is older (established 1875) and has more listed companies
- Most large caps trade on both; some small/micro caps may be BSE-only

### Trading Hours

| Session | Time (IST) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-Open** | 9:00 - 9:08 AM | Order entry, price discovery |
| **Opening** | 9:08 - 9:15 AM | Order matching, opening price calculated |
| **Normal Market** | 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM | Regular trading |
| **Closing Session** | 3:30 - 3:40 PM | Closing price calculation |
| **Post-Closing** | 3:40 - 4:00 PM | Trade modification/cancellation only |

**Key times to watch:**
- **9:15 AM**: Market opens — first 15 mins high volatility
- **12:00 PM**: Midday, often lower volume
- **2:30 PM**: US pre-market cues start affecting
- **3:30 PM**: Closing — institutional orders often execute

## Sector Overview — Indian Economy

### Key Sectors by GDP Contribution

| Sector | GDP Weight | Key Companies | Growth Drivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Services (IT/BPO)** | ~55% | TCS, Infosys, HCL Tech | Global digital transformation, AI |
| **Manufacturing** | ~17% | L&T, Tata Steel, JSW | PLI schemes, China+1, Make in India |
| **Agriculture** | ~18% | ITC (agri), UPL, PI Industries | Monsoon, export demand |
| **Banking/Finance** | ~20% (financial sector) | HDFC Bank, SBI, Bajaj Finance | Credit growth, digital lending |
| **Real Estate** | ~6-7% | DLF, Godrej Properties | Urbanization, REITs, affordable housing |
| **Auto** | ~7% | Maruti, Tata Motors, M&M | EV transition, scrappage policy |

### Consumption Story

India's consumption theme plays out through:
- **Urban consumption**: Premium products, QSR, EVs — Titan, Titan Company, Jubilant Food
- **Rural consumption**: Tractors, two-wheelers, FMCG — M&M, Hero, HUL
- **Digital consumption**: Fintech, e-commerce, SaaS — Zomato, PolicyBazaar, Zoho (unlisted)

## Key Regulatory Bodies

| Body | Role |
|---|---|
| **SEBI** | Securities and Exchange Board of India — market regulator |
| **RBI** | Reserve Bank of India — monetary policy, banking regulation |
| **Ministry of Finance** | Fiscal policy, Union Budget |
| **IRDAI** | Insurance regulator |
| **PFRDA** | Pension regulator (NPS) |

### Important SEBI Rules to Know

| Rule | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **T+1 Settlement** | Shares delivered in 1 day (India moved from T+2) |
| **Circuit Breakers** | Trading halt at -10%, -15%, -20% on NIFTY/SENSEX |
| **Stock Circuit Limits** | Individual stocks have 2%, 5%, 10%, 20% circuits |
| **F&O Expiry** | Weekly (Thursday) and monthly expiry |
| **FII Registration** | Foreign investors must register with SEBI |
| **Insider Trading** | Strict prohibition, heavy penalties |
| **Buyback** | Companies can buy back shares (tax efficient for shareholders) |

| Event | When | Market Impact |
|---|---|---|

| **Quarterly Results** | Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct (staggered) | Stock-specific moves |
| **F&O Expiry** | Last Thursday of month | High volatility |
| **US Fed Meetings** | 8 times a year | FII flows impact |
| **Monsoon Forecast** | June (IMD forecast) | Agri, FMCG, Auto |
| **Auto Monthly Sales** | 1st of every month | Auto sector stocks |
| **GST Collections** | Monthly (1st week) | Economic health indicator |
